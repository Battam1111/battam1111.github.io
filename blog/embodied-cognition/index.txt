13:T5cd,<h2 id="-what-is-embodied-cognition"><a class="anchor" href="#-what-is-embodied-cognition">🤖 What is Embodied Cognition?</a></h2>
<p>Embodied cognition argues that <strong>mind is not separate from body</strong> — our thoughts arise from our physical interaction with the world.</p>
<p>AI researchers have often ignored embodiment in favor of symbolic or disembodied models. But recent advances in robotics and sensorimotor learning are changing this.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="-why-agents-need-bodies"><a class="anchor" href="#-why-agents-need-bodies">🌍 Why Agents Need Bodies</a></h2>
<p>Without a body, agents lack:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Perceptual grounding</strong></li>
<li><strong>Physical causality</strong></li>
<li><strong>Temporal continuity</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>This leads to brittle generalization and a lack of <strong>common sense</strong>.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="-neuroscience-agrees"><a class="anchor" href="#-neuroscience-agrees">🔬 Neuroscience Agrees</a></h2>
<p>Studies of the human brain show that sensory-motor regions are deeply involved in abstract reasoning. Even language comprehension activates <strong>motor neurons</strong>.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="-the-future"><a class="anchor" href="#-the-future">🧭 The Future</a></h2>
<p>LLMs + robots ≠ sci-fi anymore.</p>
<p>The most robust agents will be those that <strong>live, move, and learn</strong> in the real world.</p>
