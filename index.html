PhD Candidate, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University · Research Intern, Microsoft Research Asia, Singapore

Models get better by training. The environments that train them get better only when someone rewrites them. I want environments that improve the way models do.

NowAgentic RL on long-horizon tasks, and how an environment can improve with training instead of staying fixed: a flawed one teaches the agent its flaws. I wrote up one case in Crooked Environments, Crooked Agents.
